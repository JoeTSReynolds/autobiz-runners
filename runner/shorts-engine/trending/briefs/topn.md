# Top N times <person> <did the theme>

A countdown of the person doing one recognisable thing (dodging a question, roasting someone, losing their temper, being unexpectedly funny), in their own words, ending on why they are in the news this week.

- `title`: "Top 5 times <name> <theme>" (the number is fixed later to the verified count, 4 or 5).
- You never need exact timings: copy the words and the code finds each word's time in the captions. The `[start-end]` times in `transcripts` only help you find the passage; a long line is fine.
- `moments`: 6 to 8 candidates, best first (always at least 6: moments where the person is off camera are dropped, and the spares fill in). Prefer moments where the person is on camera while they speak. Each is one clip from one source: `fromQuote` is the first words of the clip (the question or setup, 3 to 8 words copied exactly), `toQuote` the last words (the punchline or answer, 3 to 8 words copied exactly), in that order in the same source, 2.5 to 12 seconds apart (the clip then runs on through the reply). `label`: 2 to 5 words on screen (e.g. "I don't know"). Every moment must really show the theme; a joke that is not the theme does not count. The moments are separate times: take them from at least 2 different videos (one interview cut into five is one moment, not five).
- `closer`: `headline` is the title of one of `person.headlines` (copied exactly) that says why they are trending; `line1` is a short all-caps line from it ("THIS WEEK PIERCE BROSNAN CALLED HIM 'SO STRONG'"), `line2` a short tease with at most one emoji ("STILL DODGING? 👀").
